Build 2026 · From idea to build

Applications are open.

Two phases. Apply online first, then build in person. Free to enter, open to everyone.

Apply

14 Sep – 10 Oct 2026

Online. Free. Open to everyone.

  • Bring an idea, a problem or a skill — nothing finished required.
  • Come solo or with a team. Every kind of builder is welcome.

Developers, designers, founders, researchers, students and operators are all welcome. Already have a prototype? Add a public link in the optional work-link field. No demo or pitch deck needed.

Submit your idea →
Physical build

24–25 Oct 2026

In person at Your Venue. Free entry.

  • A focused 24-hour hackathon to turn your idea into something real.
  • Build solo, or with a team of 2–4 (four is the max).

The physical build is for selected builders. Build space and internet are provided. Food and accommodation are available at a small participant fee, shared before you travel.
